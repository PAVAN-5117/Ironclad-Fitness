Our Story

Where Passion Meets Precision

Founded in Mumbai in 2014, IRONCLAD FITNESS was born from a singular vision — to bring a world-class, luxury fitness experience to India. Every square foot of our facilities is meticulously designed to inspire, motivate, and empower.

We don't just build bodies; we build lifestyles. From our imported Eleiko and Technogym equipment to our spa-grade recovery zones, every detail reflects our obsession with excellence.

World-Class Facilities

Designed for Peak Performance

Every corner of IRONCLAD is engineered to deliver an unparalleled fitness experience with equipment sourced from the world's finest brands.

Strength Arena

Over 200+ pieces of premium strength equipment from Hammer Strength, Eleiko, and Rogue. Olympic platforms, competition-grade racks, and a dedicated powerlifting zone.

Cardio Deck

Panoramic views with Technogym Artis treadmills, Concept2 rowers, Assault bikes, and SkiErgs. Each station features personal entertainment screens with Netflix and Spotify integration.

Functional Zone

3,000 sq.ft. open turf area with sleds, battle ropes, plyo boxes, kettlebells, and TRX stations. Perfect for HIIT, CrossFit-style WODs, and functional training.

Yoga & Meditation Studio

A serene, temperature-controlled studio with bamboo flooring, ambient soundscapes, and infrared panels for hot yoga. Guided meditation sessions included.

Hydrotherapy & Spa

Luxury recovery suite featuring a 25m lap pool, steam room, Finnish sauna, ice plunge pool, and Jacuzzi. Post-workout deep tissue massage available on request.

Nutrition Lounge

In-house café with macro-tracked meals prepared by certified nutritionists. Fresh cold-pressed juices, protein shakes, and a curated supplement bar.
